Title: Deep Residual Learning for Visual Recognition and Tool-Augmented Agents

Abstract: We present a comprehensive methodology for autonomous tool-use and neural reasoning. The primary methodology of this paper utilizes reinforcement learning fine-tuning combined with Monte Carlo tree search (MCTS) to guide chain-of-thought exploration across multi-step API environments.

1. Methodology and Architecture:
The proposed architecture consists of three distinct phases: First, dense semantic retrieval via sentence transformers; Second, iterative chain-of-thought decomposition where complex queries are broken into atomic tool invocations; Third, an actor-critic verification framework that validates API response schemas before final answer generation.

2. Experimental Results:
Our experiments demonstrate a 42% improvement in multi-hop question answering accuracy compared to baseline standard prompting.
